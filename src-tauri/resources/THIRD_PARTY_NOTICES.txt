Panda Gaming Desktop - Third-Party Software Notices
===================================================

Panda Gaming Desktop distributes the official portable scrcpy package so
that users can mirror and control Android devices without installing adb or
scrcpy separately. The exact bundled package, version, source URL and SHA-256
digest are recorded in tools/panda-tools.json inside the application bundle.

The following notices apply to components that may be included in that
